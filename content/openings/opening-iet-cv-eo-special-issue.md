Submit your paper to the **IET Image Processing** special issue on **Computer Vision for Earth Observation and Environmental Monitoring**. Topics include image/video processing, deep learning for remote-sensing data, multispectral and hyperspectral imaging, multi-modality data processing, and integration with other data sources for environmental decision-making.

**Editors**: Chunbo Luo (Exeter, lead) · Keiller Nogueira (Liverpool) · Paolo Russo (Sapienza University of Rome) · Fabiana Di Ciaccio (Florence) · Chao Zhou (China University of Geosciences).
