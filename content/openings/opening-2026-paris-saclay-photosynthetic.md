Joint scholarship between the **University of Exeter** and **Université Paris-Saclay**. Develop a machine-learning model to learn an integrated latent representation of canopy functional state, analysed in relation to meteorological variables to characterise Gross Primary Productivity (GPP) responses to climatic forcing.

- **Funding**: Exeter scholarship equal to UKRI rate (£20,780 p.a. tax-free, 2025-26 rate), indexed annually, for up to 3.5 years.
- **Research costs**: consumables, workshops, training; £2,500 travel allowance.
- **Mobility**: at least twelve months at the partner institution.
- **Start**: September 2026.
- **Supervisors**: Chunbo Luo (Exeter) and Fabienne Maignan (Paris-Saclay).
