The University of Exeter is offering **50 CSC-funded PhD projects** this cycle, jointly with the China Scholarship Council. We welcome applicants in the ISE Group's areas — including **AI / Foundation Models**, **signal processing**, and **IoT-related research**.

If you are interested, get in touch early. I will guide you through the application directly — including materials preparation, sample documents (research proposal, personal statement), and the choice of research direction.

Deadline: **December 2025** (check the official scheme page for the exact date).
