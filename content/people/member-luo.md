Addressing the climate emergency and ecological crisis (University of Exeter Strategy 2030 priority) increasingly relies on AI-driven environment intelligence built upon trustworthy environmental observations, yet such data are often scarce due to unstable or disrupted environmental conditions. My research aims to build intelligent sensing and networking systems for remote sensing with exceptional coverage and resolution, bridging the gap from data scarcity to sufficiency. This vision is rooted in long-term research centred on autonomous and networked vehicles that has pushed the frontiers of remote sensing.
