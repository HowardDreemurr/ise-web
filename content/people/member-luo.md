Addressing the climate emergency and ecological crisis (University of Exeter Strategy 2030 priority) increasingly relies on AI-driven environment intelligence built upon trustworthy environmental observations, yet such data are often scarce due to unstable or disrupted environmental conditions. My research aims to build intelligent sensing and networking systems for remote sensing with exceptional coverage and resolution, bridging the gap from data scarcity to sufficiency. This vision is rooted in long-term research centred on autonomous and networked vehicles that has pushed the frontiers of remote sensing.

**Recognition.** Over **120 papers** in high-impact venues (70%+ in prestigious journals such as IEEE Transactions, RSE) and top-tier conferences (NeurIPS, AAAI, CVPR, ICC, ICASSP, EGU). **h-index 31**, **i10-index 59**, **4,144 citations**. Three Web of Science Highly Cited Papers.

**Funding.** Over **£12.5 million** in external research funding from UKRI, Horizon Europe, Innovate UK, and the Royal Society — including coordination of large-scale EU-funded projects.

**Supervision.** Currently leading a growing research group: **17 postgraduate researchers** (6 graduated) and **6 postdoctoral researchers** (4 completed). Teaching has been recognised through multiple nominations, including **Best Lecturer** and the **Students' Guild Teaching Awards**.
