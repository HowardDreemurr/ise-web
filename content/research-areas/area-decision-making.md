  The Decision Making theme closes the loop from observation to action. We combine multimodal predictions, uncertainty quantification, and domain-specific decision frameworks to drive real-world outcomes: triggering early warnings for landslides, prioritising woodland-creation investment, optimising irrigation in rural communities, and quantifying climate-tipping risks. This pillar is where research outputs become impact. Anchor projects include INSTANT (landslide prediction), Wildfire Risk Minimisation with IBM/Met Office, NEOM Vegetation Change Detection, and REFINE (air-quality monitoring).
