The 3rd ML4EO conference (18–20 June 2025) ran in Exeter — hands-on IBM Foundation Models training, NEODAAS training on processing real-world remote-sensing data, academic and industry sessions, and keynotes from the Met Office, Alan Turing Institute, Cambridge, IBM, and Exeter.
